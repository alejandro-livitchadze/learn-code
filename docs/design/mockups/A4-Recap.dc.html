Pin this to your brain
  1. 1

    A join returns one row per matching pair, not one row per left-hand row.

  2. 2

    Values from the “one” side repeat for every match on the “many” side, so sums over them grow.

  3. 3

    To report per order, group by the order, then count the many side.
